## 3. Data Handling Guidelines & Incident Escalation

If any staff member suspects that personal data has been exported to an unverified location or transmitted over unencrypted communication channels, they are obligated to inform the security incident response team immediately. 

Reports should be submitted via email to privacy-incident@corporate-domain.org or logged directly with the security desk at 800-555-3344. For escalation regarding executive records, contact Security Director Michael Torres directly at m.torres@corporate-domain.org or on his mobile line at 555-771-0092.

### 3.1 Encryption Mandate for Data in Transit

All file transfers containing user exports must utilize AES-256 bit encryption protocols. Plain text transmission of email addresses such as user.backup@internal-tech.net, payroll-admin@finance-dept.com, or hr-desk@corporate-domain.org is strictly prohibited under updated policy directives.

Furthermore, remote access connections originating from external IP ranges must be authenticated through multi-factor tokens. The IT Helpdesk line at 800-555-0199 is available twenty-four hours a day to assist staff members experiencing authentication issues or token synchronization errors.

## 4. Policy Compliance Timeline and Next Steps

The compliance review committee will convene next month to audit the remediation progress across all departmental databases. Department heads failing to achieve full masking compliance for all personal identifiers, telephone numbers, and email accounts will be subject to formal review by the executive committee.

All technical inquiries regarding schema modification scripts or masking functions should be directed to database-leads@internal-tech.net or by calling 555-220-4918 during standard business hours.
